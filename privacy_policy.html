Privacy Policy — Water Sort Puzzle

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

Thank you for playing Water Sort Puzzle. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and play our mobile application.

1. Local Game Data Storage

Your level progression, unlocked 3D glass vessels, studio background themes, coin balance, and audio/accessibility preferences are stored locally on your device using standard application storage (localStorage). We do not operate external servers that collect or store your personal name, email address, or phone number.

2. Internet Connectivity & Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Water Sort Puzzle is an online, ad-supported game and requires an active internet connection to play and load in-game rewards. We use Google AdMob as our advertising partner to display Interstitial and Rewarded Video advertisements.

To provide relevant ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain non-personal and device-level data, including:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) and device model/OS version.
  • Coarse Location Data: Approximate geographical region inferred from your IP address.
  • App Interactions & Diagnostics: Ad views, taps, crash logs, and performance telemetry.

You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms. Users can reset their Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in their Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Data Deletion & User Controls

Because all game progress and preferences are saved locally on your device, you can permanently delete all stored game data at any time by opening the in-game Settings menu and tapping Reset Save Data, or by clearing the application storage / uninstalling the app from your Android device.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for a general audience (aged 13 and older) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to comply with Google Play Store and regulatory requirements. If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on our official Google Play Store listing page.
